How long until each read is conclusive

Can I trust this week's numbers?

How to read it. Every metric is pooled across the cohort (never a per-store weekly verdict). Pre = 8 weeks before the first par change. DiD = (Test now minus Test pre) minus (Control now minus Control pre); it strips out demand, weather and seasonality that hit both arms. Waste is implied: units invoiced minus units sold in POS, never the store waste log. Sell-out before 2pm uses the calibrated POS last-sale rule (last in-store sale 2+ hours before close, volume floor 36 donut / 120 munchkin, waste buffer 24 / 60).

Pass/fail lines. Only two: pooled pre-2pm sell-outs at or under 2.5% donut / 4.6% munchkin (two consecutive weeks over = add back one dozen on the offending day-of-week pars), and bakery units within ±2% of control. Waste and net $ have expected levels that move with the dial; they are targets, not gates.
